Practical reading for Australian homeowners planning outdoor works. Published 3 November 2026.

Planning a garden that uses less water

Start with plants suited to your local climate and group plants with similar watering needs. Improve soil water retention and mulch garden beds. Discuss irrigation and maintenance needs before choosing the final layout.

Source: YourHome: outdoor water use.

Outdoor power: what to consider before landscaping

Identify underground pipes and cables before excavation. Keep electrical appliances and extension leads away from water, and involve a qualified electrical professional when planning outdoor power and lighting. Site conditions and local requirements need professional assessment.

Source: Energy Safe Victoria: electrical safety outdoors.

Think about runoff before choosing paving

Hard surfaces influence where rainwater flows. Garden areas and permeable surfaces can help slow runoff, but the right approach depends on your site. Discuss drainage early and check local council requirements before changing the way water leaves your property.

Source: YourHome: stormwater.
